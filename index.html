Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how YBC BOT ("the Bot", "we", "us", or "our") may collect, use, store, and protect information when you interact with YBC BOT on Discord.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use, YBC BOT may process limited information needed to provide its services, including:

  • Discord user IDs, usernames, and display names.
  • Discord server (guild) IDs and channel IDs.
  • Bot command usage and interaction data.
  • Server-specific settings and configuration.
  • Moderation records when moderation features are used.
  • Message content only when a feature requires the Bot to read or respond to that content.

YBC BOT does not intentionally request passwords, payment-card details, or other highly sensitive personal information.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to operate bot commands, save server settings, provide moderation and security functions, prevent abuse or spam, diagnose errors, maintain reliability, and improve YBC BOT.

4. Data Storage

Some data may be stored for as long as reasonably necessary to provide YBC BOT features, maintain server settings, prevent abuse, or comply with applicable legal obligations. Reasonable safeguards are used to help protect stored information, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Sharing

YBC BOT does not sell or rent personal information. Information may be shared only when required by law, necessary to investigate abuse or security threats, required by infrastructure or service providers that help operate the Bot, or when a feature requested by a user or server administrator requires a third-party service.

6. Discord

YBC BOT operates through Discord. Your use of Discord is also governed by Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Discord may independently collect and process information when you use its platform.

7. Data Deletion Requests

Users and server administrators may request deletion of stored information associated with them or their server. Requests can be made through the official YBC community or by contacting the YBC BOT administration team. Data may be retained where reasonably necessary for legal, security, or abuse-prevention purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

YBC BOT is intended only for users who meet Discord's minimum age requirements and any additional age requirements that apply in their location. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from users who are not permitted to use Discord.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when YBC BOT features, data practices, service providers, or legal requirements change. The latest version published on this page will replace previous versions.

10. Contact

For questions, privacy concerns, or data-deletion requests, contact the YBC BOT administration team through the official YBC community or the project's GitHub account.
